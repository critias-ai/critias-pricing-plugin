# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/critias-ai/critias-pricing-plugin/discussions).
- **Bugs:** [open an issue](https://github.com/critias-ai/critias-pricing-plugin/issues/new/choose) with your AI host and version, the plugin version, what you expected and what happened.
- **Security problems:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Never share real business data. Use synthetic or redacted figures.
